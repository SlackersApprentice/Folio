Branding

Perfume Business Packaging

A compact tool system designed to make everyday repair and project work feel lighter, safer, and more intuitive.

Role Concept design, 3D modeling
Year 2024
Focus Packaging design, brand identity

Challenge

The tool category tends to prioritize power and durability at the expense of comfort. This project rebalanced the equation around hand feel, safety, and everyday usability.

Solution

I focused on ergonomic grip geometry, balanced weight distribution, and a cleaner modular architecture that felt approachable without compromising performance.

Design process

  • Research and analysis of the target market and brand identity.
  • Developed quick concept sketches.
  • Used CAD for visuals.
  • lorem10

Outcome

Summit Pro shifted the brand story toward practical confidence and comfort, creating a tool system that felt premium without becoming heavy or intimidating.
