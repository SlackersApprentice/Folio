Branding

TG Fragrances

A fragrance brand and packaging concept designed to create a premium, memorable identity with a modern luxury feel.

Role Brand identity, packaging design
Year 2024
Focus Luxury fragrance rebrand

Challenge

The brand needed a cleaner, more elevated identity that could stand out in a crowded fragrance market while still feeling true to its story, audience, and product quality.

Solution

I developed a refined packaging and visual system around premium materials, clear brand cues, and a more luxurious overall product presence.

Design process

  • Reviewed the target market and brand positioning.
  • Explored visual directions for fragrance packaging and identity.
  • Refined bottle and label concepts through visual mockups.
  • Developed a cohesive brand mood that felt premium and contemporary.

Outcome

TG Fragrances now has a more confident and aspirational visual identity, with packaging designed to feel desirable, polished, and memorable.
