Challenge

Nature hair care concept.

Solution

I designed a family of surfaces and supports with adjustable proportions, integrated cable routing, and a minimal profile that felt intentional in residential settings.

Design process

  • Observed user behavior in collaborative and home office settings.
  • Developed concept sketches to explore proportion, utility, and storage.
  • Modeled form studies in CAD to refine balance and assembly.
  • Material review to define finish, durability, and tactile quality.

Outcome

Flux balanced comfort and adaptability, creating a furniture language that felt refined enough for living spaces and practical enough for daily work.
