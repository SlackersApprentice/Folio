Service design

ID Queue Frustrations

A service concept created to reduce queues and improve the overall experience for citizens interacting with government services.

Role Service concept, system thinking
Year 2023
Focus Accessible public service experience

Challenge

Government services often carry a negative reputation, and long queues intensify public frustration. In a digital age, many users are already active online, so a more convenient and transparent service model is needed.

Solution

The concept introduces online service alternatives, digital verification tools, better signage, and delivery-based support to reduce waiting time and improve the public experience.

Design process

  • Reviewed the public-service journey and points of friction.
  • Mapped how digital channels can reduce queue pressure and confusion.
  • Explored operational improvements such as fingerprint verification and clearer wayfinding.
  • Developed a service model that supports both convenience and trust.

Outcome

The proposal balances efficiency with empathy, creating a more respectful and accessible public service experience.
