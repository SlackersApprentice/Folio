sERVICE

ID QUEUE FUSTRATIONS

A service concept created to reduce queues and improve the overall experience for citizens interacting with government services.

Role Industrial concept, seating ergonomics
Year 2023
Focus Comfortable shared seating

Challenge

The government services already have a not so good reputation amongst its citizens. Reducing the interaction that they have to make with the services would give them time to vent the’r fustrations elswhere. Moreover, we live in an internet age. The location of the office is conveniently near many internet cafes, print shops and photo shops. Many people use social media, it would be easier to reach them. A small campaign with a series of websites of popuplar social media sites can be run as a short promotion of the service.

Solution

To reduce the queues significantly and improve the service, the service should have an online alternative such as a website. Additionally, incorporating the use of digital fingerprint scanners,signages and delivery services.

Design process

  • Reviewed seating posture and social use across work and rest scenarios.
  • Created form studies to tune seat angle, back support, and arm balance.
  • Developed CAD iterations to evaluate assembly and material thickness.
  • Evaluated finish selections and visual softness in presentation renders.

Outcome

The chair concept translated comfort and visual calm into a refined, retail-ready furniture story with strong workplace relevance.
