Product Design

Cosmetics Sticks & Brush Holder

A compact storage concept designed to organise beauty tools in a tidy, attractive, and practical way.

Role Industrial design, product concept
Year 2023
Focus Beauty storage

Challenge

Daily beauty routines often create clutter, especially when products and brushes are not stored in a clear, accessible way. The brief focused on reducing mess while keeping the product visually appealing for a bathroom or vanity surface.

Solution

I developed a compact holder that separates cosmetics and brushes in a balanced, easy-to-use arrangement while still feeling refined and elegant in a home setting.

Design process

  • Explored everyday beauty habits and storage pain points.
  • Developed concept sketches around organisation, balance, and access.
  • Refined form and proportion through digital studies.
  • Considered material feel, durability, and aesthetic clarity for home use.

Outcome

The final concept brings a cleaner, more thoughtful approach to beauty storage, combining practicality with a softer, more decorative identity.
