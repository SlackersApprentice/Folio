Product Design

Cosmetics Sticks & Brush Holder

A compact speaker concept for everyday rooms, designed to balance calm aesthetics with rich, personal sound.

Role Industrial design, material direction
Year 2023
Focus Cosmetics

Challenge

The target area is a place where abundant used/ discarded materials could be gathered e.g bars. To reduce the number of materials that go to the landfill and is littered in the environment.• Not a lot of people would be open to the idea of used/ refurbished products. • Not a lot of people would be open to the idea of used/ refurbished products.

Solution

I developed a rounded form with subtle detailing and a balanced silhouette, pairing acoustic performance with intuitive daily use.

Design process

  • Explored how sound quality translates into product form and volume.
  • Created concept sketches around minimal sculptural language.
  • Worked through CAD iterations to tune proportions and material transitions.
  • Evaluated texture, surface detailing, and lifestyle positioning.

Outcome

Mira gave the project a more emotional and atmospheric identity while still feeling practical and highly usable in home environments.
