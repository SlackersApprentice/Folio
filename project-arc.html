Mobility

All Trolley

An assistive trolley concept designed to make carrying groceries easier, safer, and more comfortable for daily use.

Role Concept design, CAD
Year 2024
Focus Assistive mobility design

Challenge

Carrying heavy groceries puts strain on the arms, shoulders, and hands, especially for older users or people with limited strength. The everyday task can feel physically demanding and inconvenient.

Solution

I designed a practical trolley concept that supports the load more evenly, reduces physical effort, and feels approachable for everyday grocery trips.

Design process

  • Studied common grocery-carrying pain points and movement patterns.
  • Explored form language through sketches and early concept studies.
  • Modeled the trolley frame in CAD to refine balance, usability, and proportion.
  • Aligned material and finish choices with practical, durable everyday use.

Outcome

The final concept delivers a simple, supportive aid that makes a routine task feel lighter, easier, and more inclusive.
