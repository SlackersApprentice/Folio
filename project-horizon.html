Upcycled furniture

Khaya Task Chair

A task chair designed to upcycle discarded materials into a sustainable seating solution.

Role Concept design, ergonomics, prototyping
Year 2025
Focus Work / Study experience

Challenge

Poor waste management is an issue in Botswana A significant sum of plastic bottles end up in visible places, contributing to environmental degradation. This project aims to address this issue by upcycling plastic bottles into functional and aesthetically pleasing furniture for the target market

Solution

I designed a study/work chair made from upcycling and recycling materials for home use.

Design process

  • Research into kitchen rituals and daily friction points.
  • Sketch exploration for modularity, proportion, and interaction.
  • CAD development to test assembly, material logic, and ergonomics.
  • Prototype and review to refine tactile details and manufacturing efficiency.

Outcome

The system balanced brand desirability with production practicality, giving the concept a clear point of view while remaining usable in real homes.
